- three world stages
- stage saved data and network sync
- dragon kill -> Stage 2
- dragon egg element ritual framework
- water and air element items
- Air Temple / Gale Core Air Element path
- Stage 3 End portal redirect to Aender

- Aender water flow changes
- Aender weather blocking
- Aender chunk volatility/stability foundation
- Aenderite ore/raw/ingot material foundation
- horizontal Aender portal, automatic counterparts, and 8:1 Overworld travel scaling
- Endermen stop eye-contact aggression in Stage 2/3
- undead stop burning/fearing sun in Stage 2
- undead and zombified piglins are cleansed/blocked in Stage 3
- living piglins are blocked before Stage 3 and enabled/immune in Stage 3
- villager teaching and recipe knowledge system
- advancement hiding / recipe book knowledge gating
- delayed mansions and outposts until Stage 2

- complete tools/armor/ores progression rework

- sword blocking/combat rework
- full villager economy/trade redesign
- full stage-specific Nether escalation including wildfires and possible Nether dragon
- possible player-facing Stage 3 illager escalation clarity beyond existing vanilla/AI support

- C418/music-disc monster
- structure removals and boss temple content beyond current delayed structure work

## Aender

| Design item | Status | Current implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Aender is sky-like/floating islands | Implemented / needs verification | `AenderChunkGenerator`, `AenderIslandSampler`, `AenderIslandArchetype`, `AenderDecorationPlanner`, `AenderCavePlanner`, `AenderCaveCarver`, `AenderOrePlanner`, `AenderBiomeSource`, Aender biome/data/assets. Fresh worlds select generator V3: V2's order-independent overlap composition and chunk-halo decoration plus deterministic per-island 3D biome assignment. Plains and the experimental inverted Aender Desert own separate archetype families, surface/underside profiles, vertical distribution, erosion, warped detached satellites, decoration patches, rare spire/crater formations, and conservative cave networks in substantial stable silhouettes. Large eligible islands always receive at least one valid main passage and often receive multiple passages. Main routes use asymmetric turns and local meanders, while most can add a connected enclosed side branch and some branch ends widen into chambers. Each main passage can independently open at either or both ends through a restrained surface, side, or underside entrance. Fragile/open archetypes remain excluded to avoid arch-like results. Stacked islands can resolve to different biomes and volatile regeneration replaces biome containers together with terrain. Diamond-like Aenderite deposits cross chunk borders and favor island undersides; most veins contain 3–4 or 4–8 blocks, rare veins contain 8–12. Full-pipeline GameTests confirm cave carving and ore placement. Upgraded saves retain their persisted V1/V2 generator selection. Pure planners have deterministic coverage, while V3 palettes, every silhouette/profile/formation, cave appearance/entrances/branches, Aenderite discoverability/exposure, vertical biome transitions, client models, performance, and chunk borders still need multi-seed real-dimension visual verification. |
| Dimension of change / chunks regenerate differently | Implemented / needs verification | `AenderRealityData` persists the seed/global/regional epochs/generator version, while the `retold:aender_chunk_reality` attachment persists each chunk's current or stale generation identity. Saved placed/broken blocks therefore survive runtime-cache loss, quit, and restart until their region legitimately changes; unloading evicts only the duplicate runtime signature, which is restored from the attachment on demand. In multiplayer, 384-block generator regions independently advance after their final client watcher leaves; a dependency halo prevents cross-region island seams. Stale loaded chunks enter a TPS-paced blanking queue only while unwatched, their non-player entities are discarded before the temporary empty interval, stabilized chunks are preserved, and rebuilding expands from approaching players. Chunk-load callbacks and portal lookup no longer synchronously rebuild stale terrain. Tests cover serialization, attachment state transitions and cache restoration, entity cleanup, watcher lifecycle, and stable-versus-volatile policy. A three-start dedicated-server check on an isolated affected-world copy verified place/save/reload and break/save/reload; rapid multiplayer re-entry, crash recovery, and the revised TPS profile still need real-dimension verification. |
| Stabilizer block makes chunks permanent | Implemented / needs verification | `AenderStabilityData`, `AenderStabilizerEvents`, `aender_stabilizer`. GameTests cover codec round trips and overlapping stabilizer counts; real-dimension behavior still needs in-game verification. |
| Water flows faster/farther | Implemented | `AenderWaterFluidMixin`, `AenderFlowingFluidMixin`. |
| Lava cannot be placed there | Implemented / needs in-game verification | `AenderBucketItemMixin` makes lava bucket emptying in the Aender vaporize like water in the Nether, consuming the bucket action while leaving no lava block behind. |

| Portal charge prepares destination terrain | Implemented / needs performance verification | Survival/adventure charge for at least 100 ticks; all players entering the Aender remain in the portal beyond their configured minimum when necessary until the 5x5 destination core is loaded/current and every already-loaded destination-view chunk is current, stable, or blank. `AenderPortalWarmup` uses a portal ticket plus an average-tick-time-aware budget capped at 12 ms/32 chunks per tick and continues regenerating the wider view opportunistically without technical action-bar text. Normal portal travel performs no synchronous Aender chunk generation and cannot reveal deletion in progress; a bounded synchronous fallback remains only for commands, other integrations, and non-player entities that bypass the warm-up. |
